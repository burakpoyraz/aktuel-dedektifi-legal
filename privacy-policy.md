# Privacy Policy — Aktüel Dedektifi

**Last updated:** May 2026  
**Operated by:** Burak Poyraz (burak.poyraz32@gmail.com)

## Overview
Aktüel Dedektifi is a private automation tool that monitors Turkish 
retail market catalogs (BİM, A101, ŞOK) and publishes short-form 
video content to social media platforms including TikTok.

## Data We Collect
- **TikTok OAuth access token** — stored locally on the operator's 
  server, never transmitted to third parties
- **Video content** — generated automatically from public catalog data
- **No personal data** from end users is collected

## How We Use Data
- Access tokens are used exclusively for authenticating API requests 
  to TikTok on behalf of the authorized account owner
- Video content is published to the operator's own TikTok profile

## Data Storage
All tokens and data are stored locally on a private server 
controlled by the operator. No cloud storage or external databases 
are used.

## Data Sharing
We do not sell, trade, or share any data with third parties.

## Data Retention
Access tokens are stored until manually deleted by the operator.

## Your Rights
As this application operates on a single authorized account, 
the account owner may revoke access at any time through 
TikTok's app settings.
